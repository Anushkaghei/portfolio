01 · About

Engineer first, researcher by habit.

I'm a graduate student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University at Buffalo, specializing in AI and machine learning. Right now I split my time between shipping product at Offerwell, a real-estate transaction platform, and building the analysis pipeline for an NIA-funded sleep and cognition study at Weiss Labs.

Before UB I interned at Siemens Healthineers, Opkey, Deloitte, and PwC, working on Kubernetes data pipelines, LLM agents, recommendation models, and analytics. I've published five papers on NLP, multimodal learning, computer vision, and simulation.

What ties it together: I care about systems that are correct when nobody is watching. That means authorization enforced on the server, retries that don't create duplicates, and models whose predictions can be explained.
